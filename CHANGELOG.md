# Changelog: Economic Dossier H1 2026 Analytical Dashboard (BETA)

## Beta v1.3 (28 Oct 2026)

The dashboard now holds data views and charts only. The written report stays separate.

- Removed the embedded report pages from the Overview: Executive Summary, Assessment, Recommendations and footnotes 1 to 9.
- Removed the "Five findings" key messages taken from the report.
- The Overview is now data-led: world map, Malaysia benchmark strip, regional summary cards, themes, opportunities, trade and ASEAN highlights, and headline figures, all with short neutral captions.
- Tab 3 shows the themes raised in Malawakil recommendations as data only, with no owners, dates, missions or trade values.
- All earlier data corrections, the styling and the methods note are kept. The footer label reads Beta v1.3.

- The footer label reads Beta v1.1.

## Beta v1.0 (28 Oct 2026)

Aligned to the Economic Dossier Report H1 2026, version 9.23.

### Restyle
- Colours, fonts and headings follow the report: navy #0B1F3A, gold #C9A227, cream panels #F5F2E8, report blue #1D4E89 and Arial.
- Gold rule under the header, report-style section headings and card title bars, cream KPI panels with a gold top rule, navy table headers.
- Chart palette matches the report. Justified body text, with more space above charts.

### Content
- The report's Executive Summary, Assessment and Recommendations (pages 2 to 5, footnotes 1 to 9) are embedded at the top of the Overview.
- The Overview's key messages are replaced by the report's five findings.
- Growth is compared quarter by quarter (Malaysia Q1 5.4%, Q2 6.0%, H1 5.7%, DOSM/OpenDOSM). Malaysia is growing faster than 139 of 167 hosts, about the same as 8 and slower than 20. 19 hosts have no comparable figure.
- Labels read "Real GDP growth (year on year)".
- Coverage reads 209 dossiers from 104 Malawakil, of which 186 are national dossiers from 81 Embassies and High Commissions.
- Method notes now say only that opportunities and risks were tagged from dossier text with the support of analytical tools.
- Removed "Phase 1" and em dashes. Region is named South & Central Asia. Footer credits the Department of Policy Planning and Coordination.
- Password gates are unchanged.
